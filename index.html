02 — About

I believe great UI design is mostly invisible — it’s the absence of confusion, the speed of recognition, the satisfaction of a task completed without friction.

Based in Nigeria. Working with startups, scale-ups, and the occasional company that finally decided their product deserves better.

03 — Process
How I work

01

Discover

Understanding the problem before touching any tool. User interviews, competitive audits, stakeholder mapping — the invisible work that makes everything else faster.

02

Define

Turning research chaos into a clear design brief. What does success look like? What are we NOT designing? Constraints sharpened here save months later.

03

Design

Low fidelity to high fidelity without skipping steps. Every component earns its place. Every interaction has intention. Then test, break, and improve.

04

Deliver

Handoffs that actually work — annotated specs, component libraries, motion guidelines. I stay through launch and the first retrospective.
